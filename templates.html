Visitor data privacyYour test rows stay in this tab.

What stays private: Your browser reads the rows you paste or the file you choose. The dashboard does not upload your rows, change your file, or send the rows to me. Use fictional or anonymized data, not names, account numbers, passwords or internal ticket details.

How to clear your rows: Choose Restore sample data to clear this dashboard. Refresh or close this tab to discard the demo’s working copy for all three dashboards. Files on your device are not deleted.

Project inquiries: The email button includes the dashboard name, not your rows or file. You choose what to send.

Hosting: GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. That is separate from the data you test. GitHub’s data collection note.
